CX4 BUS v0.4.3 - NO-SPECULATIVE STATE + WRITE-FORWARD

- PIO+DMA is now the ONLY persistent authority for CX4 DRAM and IO writes.
- Core1/SIO no longer writes speculative samples into canonical CX4 state.
- Added 16-entry, 12-cycle read-after-write forwarding cache for the SNES CPU.
- Immediate RUNNING/BUSY handshake remains on core1.
- Added fwd_put, fwd_hit and sio_block diagnostics.
- Added hle_badparts guard: pathological >64-piece assemblies are skipped during bring-up.
- Keeps exact C4ConvOAM-style sprite conversion, stable 3 KiB snapshot and zero-pad ROM mapping.
- RA v2.0 WRAMSEL + READ-REPAIR logic remains unchanged.
