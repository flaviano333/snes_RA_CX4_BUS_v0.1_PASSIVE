CX4 BUS v0.3.3 - LLE FAST-BUS TRACE

- Fix principal: remove cx4_sync/core_step_master from the latency-critical SNES READ path.
- /RD, /WR and address are sampled after a short PHI2-high settling delay.
- Write data is sampled while PHI2 is still HIGH instead of after the falling edge.
- Adds CX4TRACE: last 64 IO reads/writes seen by the active responder.
- Keeps v0.3.2 ROM slot/injector and the v2.0 WRAM READ-REPAIR path.
- Still experimental: the LLE core and SNES-side bus share state across cores; this version is aimed at validating the interface/self-test timing before final synchronization.
