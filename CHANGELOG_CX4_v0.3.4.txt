CX4 BUS v0.3.4 - SINGLE-OWNER SHADOW

- Core1 no longer calls cx4_read/cx4_write.
- Core0 is the sole owner of HG51B IO/control state and execution.
- 3 KiB Data RAM uses direct byte access for low latency.
- CPU-visible IO uses a fast 1 KiB shadow published by core0.
- IO writes use a lock-free single-producer/single-consumer queue.
- GPR mirror writes are reflected immediately in the shadow.
- Fixed work scheduling: HALT/SUSPEND writes no longer spuriously start turbo work.
- Timed suspend is no longer treated as a reason to stop servicing the core.
- Turbo slice reduced from 65536 to 8192 master clocks to lower IO queue latency.
- Post-PHI2 sample delay reduced from 8 NOPs to 2 NOPs; the responder is faster.
- CX4STAT adds ioq= and ioq_drop=. ioq_drop MUST remain 0.
- RetroAchievements v2.0 WRAM/READ-REPAIR path is otherwise preserved.
- HALT ($7F53) and indefinite SUSPEND ($7F55) explicitly stop turbo servicing.
- Timed suspend ($7F56-$7F5C) continues receiving virtual cycles; RESUME ($7F5D) restarts service.
