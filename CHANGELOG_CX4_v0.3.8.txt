CX4 BUS v0.3.8 - PIO-WRITE AUTHORITY

- CX4 LLE writes are now sourced from the proven PIO+DMA write capture.
- Core1 remains the low-latency active READ responder.
- Core1 SIO write samples are speculative only for immediate readback/status.
- PIO write stream is consumed incrementally; no need to wait for 1024-sample batch completion.
- Adds CX4STAT counters: pio_w, pio_dram, pio_io, sio_shadow.
- Keeps v0.3.7 deep queue + streaming LLE + RA v2.0 READ-REPAIR.

Target symptom: v0.3.7 physical runs lost/corrupted base/PB bytes despite CX4SELF being exact.
